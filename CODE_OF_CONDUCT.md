# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect contributors to be respectful, clear, and constructive. Keep discussions focused on the work, welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience, and give feedback about behavior or code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and submitting material that violates someone else's rights.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this standard. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's configured maintainer or security reporting channel. Do not include private personal information in public issues.
